# Idea Research: Lightweight Socialization & Top Users

- **Slug**: socialization-feature
- **Created**: 2026-11-27T17:00:23+08:00
- **Evidence confidence (overall)**: high

## Users & Demand

- **Direct product owner request for lightweight socialization & top-user discovery**: The project owner asked for five connected socialization capabilities: (1) user-to-user chat, (2) friend requests, (3) sending studies directly to other users, (4) sharing studies externally to other platforms with site attribution, and (5) a left-side "top users" widget (with criteria to be determined) linking to user profiles where others can see what they interact with and view—while keeping the experience lightweight rather than competing with full-scale platforms like Reddit. — [source: .specify/assessments/socialization-feature/intake.md] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Target audience values peer credibility and literature curation**: SBL News targets lifters, strength coaches, clinicians, students, and exercise-science researchers (`public.community_role_type`: `'Lifter'`, `'Strength Coach'`, `'Exercise Researcher'`, `'Clinician'`, `'Student'`) who read and discuss peer-reviewed resistance-training papers. Highlighting active domain contributors and making it effortless to share a study card with a training partner or coach directly supports literature discovery and organic word-of-mouth growth. — [source: specs/001-sbl-news-mvp/spec.md#L10-L12, specs/003-user-profiles/data-model.md#L13-L16, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: medium, assumption)
- **No external share action or dynamic study Open Graph metadata exists today**: Neither `StudyCard` (`web/src/components/feed/study-card.tsx#L72-L90`) nor `MetadataBreakdown` (`web/src/components/study/metadata-breakdown.tsx#L159-L169`) includes a "Share" or "Send" action—only `Discussion` and `PubMed / DOI` links are rendered. Furthermore, `/study/[id]` (`web/src/app/study/[id]/page.tsx`) does not export `generateMetadata`, so pasting a `/study/[id]` URL into external platforms (X/Twitter, Discord, Reddit, iMessage, WhatsApp) falls back to the generic site-level Open Graph title in `RootLayout` (`web/src/app/layout.tsx#L27-L62`) instead of the study's title, journal, evidence badge, and takeaway. — [source: web/src/components/feed/study-card.tsx#L72-L90, web/src/components/study/metadata-breakdown.tsx#L159-L169, web/src/app/study/[id]/page.tsx#L1-L25, web/src/app/layout.tsx#L27-L62] (confidence: high, cited)
- **No production user telemetry or feature-request tickets in repository**: Because `001-sbl-news-mvp` and `003-user-profiles` were recently built, there is no quantitative user analytics or production telemetry proving demand for real-time 1:1 chat or bidirectional friend graphs versus simpler study sharing and contributor leaderboards. — [source: .specify/assessments/user-profiles/research.md#L12] (confidence: high, cited)

## Prior Art

- **Existing User Profiles & Public Commenter Views (`003-user-profiles`)**: The codebase already provides `public.profiles` (`id`, `display_name`, `avatar_url`, `affiliation_role`, `bio`, `theme_preference`, `role`, `created_at`, `updated_at`), `/profile` (authenticated settings + paginated personal Upvoted Studies and Comment History), and `/profile/[id]` (public read-only profile displaying `UserAvatar`, `display_name`, `affiliation_role`, `bio`, and paginated public Comment History). — [source: specs/003-user-profiles/spec.md#L36-L68, web/src/app/profile/[id]/page.tsx#L50-L92] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Deliberate privacy boundary in `003-user-profiles` on Upvoted Studies**: Migration `0008_user_profiles_and_storage.sql` explicitly dropped `votes_select_public` and enforced `votes_select_own` (`FOR SELECT TO authenticated USING ((select auth.uid()) = user_id)`) plus `REVOKE SELECT ON TABLE public.votes FROM anon`, while `specs/003-user-profiles/spec.md` (`FR-008`, `SC-005`) strictly prohibited exposing a user's upvoted studies list on `/profile/[id]`. The new intake request ("so others can view their profile and see what they interact and view") directly tensions with that privacy rule unless upvoted/viewed study activity is made public, opt-in, or restricted to friends. — [source: specs/003-user-profiles/data-model.md#L71-L75, specs/003-user-profiles/spec.md#L66, L91, L111] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Prior assessment warning against social-network scope creep**: `.specify/assessments/user-profiles/research.md#L38` explicitly flagged that expanding profiles into follower/friend graphs or full social-network features risks distracting from SBL News's core evidence-discovery mission and colliding with Constitution Principle V (Scoped Route Discipline). — [source: .specify/assessments/user-profiles/research.md#L38] (confidence: high, cited)
- **No view-tracking, friendship, or direct-messaging tables exist in PostgreSQL**: The current schema (`0001` through `0008`) contains `profiles`, `studies`, `guidelines`, `consensus_drift_alerts`, `comments`, `votes`, and `secondary_commentaries`. There are no tables for study views/read history, friend requests/connections, chat threads/messages, or internal study shares/notifications. — [source: specs/001-sbl-news-mvp/spec.md#L149-L157, specs/003-user-profiles/data-model.md#L119-L127] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Comparable research & technical community platforms (Hugging Face Daily Papers, Hacker News, Lobsters, Substack Notes)**: Niche literature and technical aggregators typically succeed with lightweight social proof—such as a sidebar/widget of top contributors ranked by discussion contributions or karma, rich link sharing with branded citation cards, and lightweight study recommendations/mentions—while full real-time DM chat and bidirectional friend graphs are rarely used on research feeds unless tied directly to sharing papers. — [source: ASSUMPTION] (confidence: medium, assumption)

## Market & Context

- **How users cope today**:
  - *External sharing*: Users must manually copy the browser URL for `/study/[id]` or the raw PubMed link; when pasted externally, `/study/[id]` lacks per-study Open Graph metadata or formatted citation/takeaway copy. — [source: web/src/app/study/[id]/page.tsx, web/src/components/feed/study-card.tsx] (confidence: high, cited)
  - *Discovering active community members*: Readers can only discover other users by scrolling inside individual `/study/[id]` comment threads and clicking a commenter's name (`web/src/components/study/discussion-thread.tsx`); there is no global discovery of active commenters or top contributors on `/`. — [source: web/src/app/page.tsx, specs/003-user-profiles/spec.md#L56] (confidence: high, cited)
  - *Peer-to-peer communication*: Users have no way on-platform to connect with another commenter, recommend a study to a specific user on the site, or message them. — [source: specs/001-sbl-news-mvp/spec.md, specs/003-user-profiles/spec.md] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Cost of doing nothing**: Sharing SBL News studies to external platforms looks generic (missing study-specific previews and one-click branded citation snippets), limiting organic growth; additionally, active contributors receive no visibility on the main feed, and users cannot exchange studies with peers inside the app. — [source: web/src/app/layout.tsx#L40-L61, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: medium, assumption)

## Data & Constraints

- **Constitution Principle I & IV ($0/Month Free-Tier, 5 GB Egress Cap, & ISR on `/`)**:
  - **Main Feed ISR Constraint**: `/` (`web/src/app/page.tsx#L4`) uses Incremental Static Regeneration (`export const revalidate = 3600`) within `<main className="mx-auto max-w-6xl px-4 py-6 sm:px-6">` (`web/src/app/layout.tsx#L86`). A left-side "Top Users" rail on `/` works naturally with ISR if its ranking criteria are deterministic and cacheable (e.g., aggregated public comments, comment upvotes, or contribution score refreshed hourly), whereas real-time per-user presence or unbatched view tracking would fight ISR and inflate egress. — [source: .specify/memory/constitution.md#L28, web/src/app/page.tsx#L4, web/src/app/layout.tsx#L86] (confidence: high, cited)
  - **Supabase Realtime & Egress Limits**: Supabase Free includes a 5 GB monthly egress cap, 500 MB database size cap, and 200 concurrent Realtime connections limit. Full real-time WebSocket chat or logging a database row on every passive study page view ("see what they view") across anonymous/authenticated traffic could rapidly consume database rows and egress unless strictly scoped (e.g., tracking intentional interactions like upvotes/bookmarks/comments or capping recent view history per user, and using lightweight polling or on-demand drawer fetches for messages/study-sends). — [source: .specify/memory/constitution.md#L29, L70, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: high, assumption)
- **Constitution Principle III (Neo-Brutalist Sky Blue Design System & Layout Geometry)**:
  - Principle III defines the Main Feed (`/`) layout geometry (`sticky top filter/search bar` + `high-contrast study cards`) and caps body copy at `65ch–75ch`. Currently `RootLayout` constrains `<main>` to `max-w-6xl` (`1152px`). Adding a persistent left-side "Top Users" rail alongside the study feed on desktop (collapsible or stacked on mobile) requires careful responsive grid geometry within `max-w-6xl` (or widening the outer container while keeping study cards readable) and strict adherence to Neo-Brutalist tokens (`2px solid var(--color-border)`, `var(--shadow-card)`, Sky Blue `#38BDF8` accent, Light/Dark theme parity). — [source: .specify/memory/constitution.md#L54-L61, web/src/app/layout.tsx#L86] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Constitution Principle IV & `003-user-profiles` Privacy Rules**:
  - Principle IV mandates RLS on all tables and forbids `SELECT *` on list/profile endpoints. Currently, `public.votes` is private to the voter (`votes_select_own`). If `/profile/[id]` is expanded to show what a user "interacts with and views" (e.g., upvoted studies, shared studies, or recently viewed studies), the spec and Constitution/RLS policies must explicitly define whether this activity is public for all users, opt-in via a profile privacy toggle, or visible only to accepted friends. — [source: .specify/memory/constitution.md#L70-L71, specs/003-user-profiles/data-model.md#L71-L75] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Constitution Principle V (Scoped Route Discipline — 8 Approved Routes)**:
  - Principle V strictly confines `/web` to 8 approved routes (`/`, `/study/[id]`, `/guidelines`, `/search`, `/methodology`, `/admin/review`, `/profile`, `/profile/[id]`). Any new socialization capability (friend requests, chat/study inbox, top users widget, external sharing) must either live within the existing 8 routes (e.g., left sidebar on `/`, slide-over chat/friends drawer in `TopNav`, friend/activity tabs on `/profile` and `/profile/[id]`, share menu on study cards and `/study/[id]`) OR require an explicit MINOR Constitution amendment if dedicated routes like `/messages` or `/friends` are added. — [source: .specify/memory/constitution.md#L78-L89, L123, L132-L135] (confidence: high, cited)

## Evidence Against the Idea

- **Bundling 5 distinct features risks heavy scope and moderation burden**: Combining (1) external study sharing, (2) a top-users left sidebar + expanded public profile activity, (3) a friend-request graph, (4) internal study sending, and (5) user-to-user chat in a single feature spans viral growth mechanics, layout changes, privacy model changes, and private messaging. Private user-to-user chat in particular introduces abuse, spam, blocking/reporting, and moderation requirements that do not exist for public study comments. — [source: .specify/assessments/socialization-feature/intake.md, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: high, assumption)
- **"See what they view" conflicts with lightweight free-tier architecture and reader privacy**: Recording every `/study/[id]` page view in PostgreSQL and exposing a user's passive reading history publicly on `/profile/[id]` creates both high write/egress amplification on Supabase Free and potential privacy discomfort for users who want to read studies without broadcasting their browsing history—whereas exposing *intentional interactions* (comments, upvotes, or curated/shared studies) is much lighter and higher-signal. — [source: .specify/memory/constitution.md#L70-L71, specs/003-user-profiles/spec.md#L91] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Direct conflict with `003-user-profiles` privacy requirement (`FR-008` / `SC-005`)**: `003-user-profiles` explicitly locked down `public.votes` (`votes_select_own`) so upvoted studies are private to `/profile` and excluded from `/profile/[id]`. Reversing or relaxing this requires modifying RLS policies on `public.votes` (or adding an opt-in visibility flag / friend-scoped RLS policy). — [source: specs/003-user-profiles/spec.md#L91, L111, specs/003-user-profiles/data-model.md#L71-L75] (confidence: high, cited)

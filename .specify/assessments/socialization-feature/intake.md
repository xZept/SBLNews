## Restated

Add a lightweight socialization layer to the site that supports user-to-user chat, friend requests, sharing studies both internally with other users and externally to other platforms (with attribution back to the site), and a left-side "top users" display linking to user profiles showing their interactions and viewed studies. The intent is to provide recognizable social mechanics that differentiate the site without turning it into a full-scale social network or competing directly with platforms like Reddit.

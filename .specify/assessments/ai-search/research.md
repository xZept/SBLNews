## Users & Demand

- **Direct product owner request for unified Next.js AI-assisted search (`/api/ai-search`)**: The project owner proposed exposing an authenticated AI-assisted search feature via a Next.js Route Handler (`/api/ai-search`) on Vercel instead of a separate Render free-tier backend to eliminate cold starts and keep the stack unified—combining public standard search, an "Ask AI" teaser for logged-out visitors, an atomic per-user daily quota RPC (`check_and_increment_ai_quota`, unlimited for admins), hybrid `pgvector` + full-text retrieval capped under 2,000 context tokens, a shared 7- or 30-day TTL query-answer cache in Supabase, and a 3-tier LLM failover chain (Groq -> OpenRouter `:free` -> Google Gemini). — [source: .specify/assessments/ai-search/intake.md] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Gap between multi-study training questions and card-list search results**: Today, `/search` (`web/src/app/search/page.tsx`) returns up to 20 individual `StudyCard` records and `/guidelines` (`web/src/app/guidelines/page.tsx`) covers 5 static canonical training variables (*Weekly Volume*, *RIR / Failure*, *Frequency*, *Rest Intervals*, *Protein Intake*). Readers asking synthesis questions across multiple papers (e.g., "optimal weekly sets for hypertrophy", "creatine timing", "lengthened partials in trained lifters") must read individual study cards one by one rather than receiving a synthesized, citation-grounded answer. — [source: web/src/app/search/page.tsx#L108-L282, specs/001-sbl-news-mvp/spec.md#L63-L89, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: high, cited)
- **No search input or "Ask AI" entrypoint exists on the Main Feed (`/`) today**: While `TopNav` links to `/search` (`web/src/components/layout/top-nav.tsx#L18`), `FilterBar` on `/` (`web/src/components/feed/filter-bar.tsx#L38-L100`) only renders `Daily | Weekly | Top` tabs, the `Trained Lifters Only` toggle, and study-design filter pills—there is currently no search bar or "Ask AI" toggle on the main feed. — [source: web/src/components/feed/filter-bar.tsx#L38-L100, web/src/components/feed/feed-client.tsx#L97-L107] (confidence: high, cited)
- **No production search-query telemetry or user support tickets in repository**: Because `001-sbl-news-mvp` through `004-socialization-layer` were recently built in this repository, demand for AI search synthesis is driven by product owner direction rather than observed user analytics. — [source: .specify/assessments/socialization-feature/research.md#L12] (confidence: high, cited)

## Market & Context

- **How users cope today**: Visitors must click `Search` in `TopNav` to open `/search`, enter keywords, and manually read individual 2-sentence takeaways across up to 20 study cards, or browse `/guidelines` if their question happens to match one of the 5 pre-curated training variables. — [source: web/src/app/search/page.tsx#L92-L283, web/src/app/guidelines/page.tsx] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Cost of doing nothing**:
  - Logged-out visitors on `/` see no search input or AI synthesis teaser to motivate account sign-up.
  - Signed-in users cannot get a synthesized, multi-study answer across the literature archive.
  - Hosting RAG on a separate Render Free backend would split the runtime across two hosting providers and expose users to 30–60s cold starts after idle periods. — [source: web/src/components/feed/filter-bar.tsx, .specify/assessments/ai-search/intake.md#L10-L15, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: high, assumption)

## Data & Constraints

- **Context Token Budget (<2,000 Tokens) & Supabase 5 GB Egress Cap**:
  - In `public.studies` (`supabase/migrations/0002_tables_and_indexes.sql#L15-L47`), structured study fields (`title`, `journal`, `publication_year`, `study_design`, `population`, `is_trained_population`, `sample_size_n`, `duration_weeks`, `primary_outcome`, `summary_takeaway`, `limitations`, `inspection_depth`, `quality_score`) average ~120–200 tokens per study, while `abstract_text` averages ~250–350 tokens.
  - Retrieving only the top 3–5 studies via `search_studies_hybrid` (`match_limit: 5`) and formatting their structured takeaways, primary outcomes, limitations, and truncated abstracts (capped at e.g. 800–1,200 characters per study) keeps total prompt context well below **2,000 tokens** (~800–1,600 tokens including system instructions). This allows 4–5 concurrent cache-miss LLM syntheses per minute within Groq's 8,000 TPM free-tier limit and avoids large database egress. — [source: supabase/migrations/0002_tables_and_indexes.sql#L15-L47, supabase/migrations/0004_hybrid_search_rpc.sql#L5-L43, .specify/assessments/ai-search/intake.md#L18-L19] (confidence: high, cited)
- **"Zero-Token" Retrieval Ordering & Query Embedding Nuance**:
  - While Postgres full-text search + `pgvector` distance calculation inside Supabase consumes zero LLM generation tokens, generating a 768-dim `query_embedding` for a semantic search currently invokes `generateQueryEmbedding()` against `gemini-embedding-001` (`web/src/lib/queries/embeddings.ts#L18-L33`).
  - Checking the **Shared Query-Answer Cache** *first* (before `generateQueryEmbedding()` and before or alongside quota checks) avoids even the embedding API call on cache hits. On cache misses, if `gemini-embedding-001` is unavailable or rate-limited, `search_studies_hybrid` already falls back automatically to Postgres full-text search (`query_embedding = NULL`). — [source: web/src/lib/queries/embeddings.ts#L18-L54, supabase/migrations/0004_hybrid_search_rpc.sql#L73-L117] (confidence: high, cited)
- **Vercel Hobby Execution Timeouts & 3-Tier Failover Latency**:
  - Next.js Route Handlers on Vercel Hobby must begin streaming or complete within platform timeout budgets (25s initial response timeout for streaming / 10–60s serverless execution limit).
  - Native `fetch` against OpenAI-compatible chat completions endpoints (Groq `https://api.groq.com/openai/v1/chat/completions`, OpenRouter `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions`, and Gemini's OpenAI-compatible endpoint `https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai/chat/completions` or native `generateContent`) requires Zero new npm SDK dependencies (`web/package.json`) and must enforce tight per-tier timeouts (e.g., 4–6s connect/TTFB timeout) so automatic failover on HTTP `429` or `5xx` completes rapidly across all three tiers. — [source: web/package.json#L13-L26, web/src/lib/queries/embeddings.ts#L31, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: high, assumption)

  - *Principle I ($0/Month & Stack Discipline)*: Currently states `Gemini API Free Tier (gemini-3.5-flash-lite + embeddings)` and mentions Render Free for optional decoupled RAG (`.specify/memory/constitution.md#L31-L32, L109`). Adopting Groq (`openai/gpt-oss-20b`) + OpenRouter `:free` (`nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b:free` / `nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b-reasoning:free`) + Gemini Free Tier inside `/web` (`/api/ai-search`) and noting the optional one-time $10 OpenRouter credit balance ($0/month recurring) will require a MINOR Constitution amendment if approved.
  - *Principle I & III (Main Feed ISR & Neo-Brutalist Design)*: `/` uses ISR (`export const revalidate = 3600` in `web/src/app/page.tsx#L6`). Adding an "Ask AI" toggle/button on `/` must be rendered inside client state (`FeedClient` / `FilterBar`) without turning `/` into a dynamic per-request server render, and all UI elements must follow the Neo-Brutalist Sky Blue token system (`#38BDF8` accent, `2px solid` borders, hard offset shadows, zero purple/violet hues, Light/Dark theme parity).

Adding `/api/ai-search` adds an internal API Route Handler rather than a new user-facing page route, though documenting `/api/ai-search` in the Constitution keeps route governance explicit. — [source: .specify/memory/constitution.md#L25-L90, L102-L110, web/src/app/page.tsx#L6, web/src/app/auth/callback/route.ts] (confidence: high, cited)

## Evidence Against the Idea

- **Third-party free-tier model slug churn & OpenRouter $10 balance nuance**: Free-tier model identifiers on Groq and OpenRouter (e.g., `openai/gpt-oss-20b`, `nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b:free`, `nvidia/nemotron-3-nano-omni-30b-a3b-reasoning:free`) are frequently updated, rate-limited, or retired by upstream hosts, and unlocking OpenRouter's 1,000 RPD `:free` tier requires holding a one-time $10 credit balance (even though `:free` model calls cost $0). Hardcoding model slugs instead of making them environment-configurable with sensible defaults risks brittle failovers. — [source: .specify/assessments/ai-search/intake.md#L22-L26, .specify/memory/constitution.md#L23-L32, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: high, assumption)
- **Cache staleness when new studies are published**: Caching normalized query-answer pairs for 7 or 30 days means that if a new systematic review or RCT is published during the TTL window, cached answers for recurring queries will not automatically include the new paper until the cache entry expires or is invalidated on study publication. — [source: .specify/assessments/ai-search/intake.md#L20, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: medium, assumption)
- **LLM synthesis risk vs. deterministic scientific rigor**: Free-form AI synthesis over 3–5 abstracts or takeaways can oversimplify nuanced methodological limitations if the prompt and UI do not prominently surface the underlying study cards, their deterministic evidence badges (`Meta-Analysis`, `RCT`, `Trained Population`, `Full-Text` vs. `Abstract-Only`), and study limitations. — [source: .specify/memory/constitution.md#L38-L45, ASSUMPTION] (confidence: medium, assumption)
